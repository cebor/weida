### B-031 — Bound every wait in shutdown

### B-044 — Decision 0011: how server-initiated traffic addresses a connection
kind: research | size: 45 | status: ready | needs: []
acceptance: `docs/decisions/0011-<slug>.md`, accepted, answering the question [0002](decisions/0002-control-and-bulk-separation.md) §7 left open and B-017 ran into: **a publisher writes fan-out on the connection the SUBSCRIBE arrived on, and nothing on the wire lets it choose a different one of that peer's per-path connections.** Until that is decided the control tier cannot carry SUBSCRIBE, and therefore cannot be justified at all (PROTOCOL §2.5).
